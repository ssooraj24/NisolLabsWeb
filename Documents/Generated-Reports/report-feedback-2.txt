## The Core Issue with a Pre-Pilot Document

Your current Pages 32–34 assume the pilot runs in a vacuum. They list *success thresholds* (≥95% accuracy) and *decision gates* (Gate 1–4).

## What You MUST Add (Pre-Pilot Edition)

### Addition 1: "Client-Side Dependency & Data Readiness Checklist" (MANDATORY)

Insert this **immediately before Gate 0 (Page 34)**. Make it a table that both parties sign.

> *"The 4-6 week Pilot Sprint clock shall commence only upon Nisol AI's formal written confirmation that the following client-side dependencies have been fully satisfied:"*

| Dependency | Deliverable | Responsible Party | Deadline (from Signing) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Invoice Data** | 100 historical vendor invoices (PDF/JPEG) + corresponding ERP PO numbers in CSV | Finance Controller | 5 Business Days |
| **RAG Golden Test Set** | 200 question-answer pairs verified by SMEs (e.g., "What is our VPN policy?" → "Link to page 42 of SOP.pdf") | Ops / Legal SMEs | 7 Business Days |
| **API Access** | Read-only tokens for GitHub PR diffs (Engineering) + Jira read access (PMO) | CTO / IT Ops | 10 Business Days |
| **SME Availability** | Confirmed calendar holds for the nominated SMEs (Page 7-8) for 2 hours/week | Department Heads | At Signing |
| **Cloud Environment** | Novatech-provisioned VPC / Sandbox with GPU quota approved | CTO / Cloud Ops | 10 Business Days |

**Why this is non-negotiable:** If Novatech delivers the invoices on Day 15 instead of Day 5, your pilot runs until Day 50. Your delivery pod is idle, burning cost, waiting for them. This clause **stops the clock** and protects your margin.

### Addition 2: "Pilot Scope Freeze" Clause

Your current document doesn't explicitly say: *"We are testing exactly these 3 use cases, and nothing else."* 

Department heads will inevitably say during Week 2: *"Oh, can you also make it summarize Slack threads?"* – Scope creep kills pilots.

**Add this one-sentence paragraph:**

> *"The Pilot Sprint shall evaluate strictly the Candidate Workstreams enumerated in the table on Page 33. Any additional capability requests, feature enhancements, or new departmental use cases proposed during the Pilot Sprint shall be formally logged for consideration in Wave 2+ scoping and shall not be executed within the current Pilot timeline without a mutually agreed Change Order."*

### Addition 3: "No-Blame Early Termination" Clause

This is the **ultimate trust-builder** with the CFO.

> *"At any point during the Pilot Sprint, if Novatech Systems Ltd. determines (in its sole discretion) that the AI capability is unlikely to meet the acceptance thresholds, Novatech may terminate the Pilot with 5 business days' written notice. Novatech shall retain all intellectual property generated during the Pilot (including prompts, evaluation harnesses, and test scripts) at no additional cost."*

**Why this is genius:** 
- It removes the CFO's fear of a "runaway train."
- It forces Nisol to deliver outstanding quality from Day 1.
- It positions you as a partner, not a vendor.
