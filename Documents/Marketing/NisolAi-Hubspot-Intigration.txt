For **Nisol AI**, I would not use HubSpot only as a contact database. I would use it as the **central operating system for sales and client delivery**.

### Recommended CRM Structure

#### 1. Prospects

Companies that have not yet engaged.

**Stages**

* Lead Captured
* Discovery Call Scheduled
* Discovery Call Completed
* AI Discovery Workshop Proposed
* Proposal Sent
* Negotiation
* Won
* Lost

#### 2. Clients

Once a deal is won.

**Stages**

* Discovery Workshop
* Assessment Report
* Solution Blueprint
* Implementation
* Support / Managed Services
* Upsell Opportunities

This aligns well with your Nisol Discovery methodology and AI Transformation framework.

# HubSpot Integration Architecture

## Option 1 (Recommended MVP)

### Website → HubSpot

Your website forms:

* Contact Us
* Book Discovery Workshop
* Download AI Readiness Assessment
* Download AI Playbook

send data directly to HubSpot.

Workflow:

```text
Visitor
   ↓
Website Form
   ↓
HubSpot Contact
   ↓
Lead Created
   ↓
Task Assigned
```

## Option 2 (Best for Nisol Discovery)

### HubSpot + Nisol Discovery Portal

```text
Lead
  ↓
HubSpot

Deal Won
  ↓

Nisol Discovery Portal
  ↓

Questionnaire
  ↓

AI Assessment
  ↓

AI Opportunity Matrix
  ↓

Report
  ↓

Proposal
```

The CRM manages:

* Company
* Contacts
* Meetings
* Emails
* Deals

The Nisol Discovery Portal manages:

* Questionnaire
* Readiness Assessment
* Opportunity Mapping
* ROI Calculation
* Report Generation

This separation is cleaner and more scalable. The Discovery platform is already envisioned as a dedicated client portal and assessment engine.

For the first 90 days:

### Use HubSpot for

* Lead database
* Companies
* Contacts
* Deals

* Meeting scheduling
* Tasks
* Pipeline management

### Build Nisol Discovery separately for

* AI Readiness Assessment
* Discovery Questionnaire
* Opportunity Matrix
* ROI Calculator
* AI Discovery Report
* Proposal Generator

This gives you a professional consulting workflow:

```text
Fundoodata
    ↓
HubSpot CRM
    ↓
Discovery Call
    ↓
Nisol Discovery
    ↓
Assessment Report
    ↓
Proposal
    ↓
Project Delivery
```

For a solo founder, HubSpot Free + your Nisol Discovery Portal is probably the strongest setup you can build without spending money on Salesforce or Zoho Enterprise.
