**[First Name] [Last Name]**
**[Title]**
**[Company Name]**
**[Address]**

**Re: An Invitation to Co-Create the Future of Enterprise Intelligence**

Dear [First Name],

Your competitors are spending ₹10–50 crores on AI experiments. Most of them are failing. Not because the technology isn't ready—but because they started building before they knew what to build.

We have a proposition that will sound radical: **We will deliver a complete Enterprise Intelligence Architecture for your organization in 7 days. At zero cost. In exchange for one thing: your partnership in shaping a new category.**

**What we're offering:**

Over 7 days, our team deploys the **Nisol Discovery™** framework—a forensic diagnostic powered by **RoSense AI**—to map every capability, data pipeline, and automation opportunity across your enterprise. You receive:

- **15 board-ready deliverables** (capability radar maps, DCF models, architecture blueprints, action briefs)
- **A physical, museum-quality Intelligence Dossier** (cloth-bound, foil-stamped)
- **A clear 12-month AI roadmap** with quantified ROI

**Timeline:** 7 days from kickoff to deliverable. **Investment:** ₹0. **The only cost:** Your willingness to share your success story with the world.

**Why we're doing this:**

We're launching the **Nisol Enterprise Intelligence Grant**—awarding 3 full engagements (₹4,50,000 value each) to institutions that will help us prove that enterprise intelligence doesn't require 6 months and 200-page slide decks. We've hand-selected [Company Name] because your leadership in [industry/sector] sets the standard others follow.

**What we need from you:**

A 15-minute conversation to determine if this is the right fit. No pitch. No obligation. Just a dialogue about where your AI journey stands today.

I'll have my team reach out this week to schedule. Alternatively, you can reply directly to this letter.

**One final thought:** The enterprises that win the AI race won't be the ones with the biggest budgets. They'll be the ones who started with clarity.

Let's build that clarity together.

With respect,

**[Founder/CMO Name]**
*Chief Marketing Officer, Nisol AI*

## EXECUTION NOTES

| Element | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 Targets** | Send the **physical letter** via courier. The unboxing experience is part of the pitch. |
| **Tier 2 Targets** | Use the **email version** with a Calendly link. |
| **Personalization** | Replace `[industry/sector]` with the specific vertical (BFSI, Manufacturing, Healthcare, IT/Software, Professional Services). |
| **Follow-Up Cadence** | Day 3: LinkedIn connection request. Day 7: Brief email: *"Following up on my letter—worth 15 minutes?"* Day 14: Final email: *"Closing our grant application window this week."* |
| **Physical Asset** | Include a **"Nisol Intelligence" branded pen** or a **preview card** (showing the cloth-bound dossier) inside the courier package. |
