# The Lighthouse Client Follow-Up Sequence
## 3 Emails + 1 LinkedIn Message (14-Day Cadence)

*Strategic Intent: This sequence is designed as a "high-hustle engine dressed in a brand tuxedo." It applies gentle, escalating pressure while maintaining Apple-grade composure. No desperation. No "just checking in." Every touch adds value or builds scarcity.*

---

## 📊 SEQUENCE OVERVIEW

| Touch | Channel | Day | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Touch 1** | Email | Day 1 | Deliver the invitation (value + scarcity) |
| **Touch 2** | LinkedIn DM | Day 3 | Soft ping; remind them of the letter |
| **Touch 3** | Email | Day 7 | Tease ROI; surface the cost of inaction |
| **Touch 4** | Email | Day 14 | The "Closing Window" — final urgency |

---

## TOUCH 1: EMAIL — "The Invitation" (Day 1)

*Sent immediately after the physical letter is couriered or as the initial outreach if skipping physical mail.*

---

**Subject:** An invitation: 7 days to enterprise intelligence clarity

**To:** [First Name]

Dear [First Name],

Your competitors are spending ₹10–50 crores on AI experiments. Most are failing—not because the technology isn't ready, but because they started building before they knew what to build.

We have a proposition: **We'll deliver a complete Enterprise Intelligence Architecture for [Company Name] in 7 days. At zero cost.** In exchange for your partnership in shaping a new category.

**What you receive:**
- 15 board-ready deliverables (capability radars, DCF models, architecture blueprints)
- A physical, museum-quality Intelligence Dossier (cloth-bound, foil-stamped)
- A clear 12-month AI roadmap with quantified ROI

**Timeline:** 7 days from kickoff to deliverable.
**Investment:** ₹0.
**The only cost:** Your willingness to share your success story.

We're awarding 3 full Enterprise Intelligence Grants (₹4,50,000 value each) this quarter. [Company Name] was selected because of your leadership in [industry/sector].

**Next step:** A 15-minute conversation. No pitch. No obligation. Just a dialogue about where your AI journey stands.

**Reply to this email** to schedule, or I'll have my team reach out this week.

The enterprises that win the AI race won't be the ones with the biggest budgets. They'll be the ones who started with clarity.

Let's build that clarity together.

Warmly,

**[Founder/CMO Name]**
*Chief Marketing Officer, Nisol AI*

## TOUCH 2: LINKEDIN MESSAGE (Day 3)

*Keep this ultra-short. No copy-paste. Personalize one line about their recent post / company news if possible.*

---

**Hi [First Name],**

Trust you received my letter regarding the Nisol Enterprise Intelligence Grant.

I know the pace of enterprise leadership leaves little room for "exploring" new initiatives—which is exactly why we designed a 7-day sprint instead of a 6-month consulting engagement.

If the timing isn't right, I respect that completely. But if there's even a flicker of curiosity about whether [Company Name] could benefit from a full architectural audit and a quantified AI roadmap at zero cost, I'd value 15 minutes of your time.

Either way, I'll follow up via email next week.

Warmly,
**[Your Name]**

---

## TOUCH 3: EMAIL — "The Cost of AI Confusion" (Day 7)

*This email reframes the conversation from "free offer" to "cost of inaction."*

---

**Subject:** The cost of AI confusion at [Company Name]

**To:** [First Name]

Hi [First Name],

Quick follow-up to my letter.

I realize the "free grant" might sound too good to be true. So let me reframe this in terms of what you're *already* losing by waiting:

**Every month your AI strategy remains unclear:**
- ₹2–5 crores in missed operational efficiency
- ₹1–3 crores in budget allocated to AI but not delivering measurable returns
- Precious competitive ground being ceded to peers who started earlier

The 15-capability audit we deliver in 7 days typically uncovers **₹8–15 crores** in addressable value for enterprises of your scale. We've seen it across manufacturing, BFSI, and healthcare—which is why we're so confident in offering the grant.

**Here's what we uncovered for a [similar industry] client:**
- 3 high-impact automation opportunities they were completely blind to
- ₹4.2 crores in annual savings identified and quantified
- A 12-month roadmap that turned "AI confusion" into "AI conviction"

They went from uncertainty to board-level confidence in 7 days.

**What would clarity be worth to [Company Name]?**

If this resonates, I'm keeping one slot open for you. Just reply "Yes" and I'll send over the 1-page Partnership Acknowledgment.

Warmly,

**[Founder/CMO Name]**

---

## TOUCH 4: EMAIL — "Closing the Grant Window" (Day 14)

*This is the final, respectful escalation. It creates urgency without begging.*

---

**Subject:** Closing the grant window — final call for [Company Name]

**To:** [First Name]

Hi [First Name],

I'm writing to let you know that we are finalizing the **3 Enterprise Intelligence Grant slots** for this quarter.

We've received significant interest—more than we expected, honestly—and the independent jury is preparing to select the final recipients this week.

**[Company Name]** was one of our top nominations because of your leadership in [industry/sector]. I don't want you to miss this opportunity simply because we didn't align schedules.

**If you're interested, the next step is simple:**
- Reply "Yes" to this email.
- I'll send you the 1-page Partnership Acknowledgment.
- We'll schedule your 7-day sprint to start within 10 business days.

**If this isn't the right time for [Company Name] this quarter**, I completely understand. Just reply "Not now" and we'll keep you in the loop for the next grant cycle.

**Deadline:** [Date, 3 days from now] at 5:00 PM.

I hope we get the chance to build something remarkable together.

Warmly,

**[Founder/CMO Name]**

## 📋 EXECUTION NOTES

| Element | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Best Send Times (IST/GST)** | Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday at 8:30 AM (opens before the workday begins) |
| **Subject Lines** | Keep them short (under 50 characters for mobile preview) |
| **Tracking** | Use HubSpot / Instantly to track opens. If they open 3+ times, prioritize for a personal phone call. |
| **LinkedIn Alternative** | If the LinkedIn DM goes unopened, send a **connection request** with a short note instead. |
| **Phone Call (If No Response by Day 10)** | *"Hi [First Name], it's [Your Name] from Nisol AI. I've sent a couple of emails about our Intelligence Grant. I know you're busy—I'll keep this to 60 seconds. We're selecting the final grantees this week. If there's any interest, just reply to my email. If not, I'll close your file and wish you well. Have a great day."* |
