## PART 2: THE GRANT MARKETING RIGHTS AGREEMENT
*Title: **The Nisol Enterprise Intelligence Grant — Partnership Acknowledgment***

*Subtitle: *A shared commitment to clarity, impact, and storytelling.*

---

**This Agreement is made between:**
**Nisol AI** (*"The Grantor"*) — and —
**[Recipient Organization Name]** (*"The Recipient"*)

**Date:** [Insert Date]

### 1. The Grant Scope
The Grantor agrees to provide the Recipient with a **Nisol One™ Enterprise Intelligence Discovery**, valued at **₹4,50,000 (Indian Rupees Four Lakh Fifty Thousand)** , at **zero cost** to the Recipient. This includes:
- A 7-day strategic diagnostic sprint.
- 15 board-ready capability deliverables.
- A physical, museum-quality Intelligence Dossier.

### 2. The Recipient's Commitment
In exchange for this grant, the Recipient agrees to:
- **Provide Access:** Facilitate interviews with 3–5 key stakeholders (leadership, operations, and ground staff) during the 7-day sprint.
- **Timely Feedback:** Review draft deliverables within 48 hours of receipt to ensure the final product is accurate and actionable.

### 3. Media & Content Rights (The "Storytelling" Clause)
The Recipient agrees that the Grantor may capture, produce, and distribute content related to this engagement for promotional, educational, and brand-building purposes. This includes, but is not limited to:

- **Written Content:** A detailed case study published on the Grantor’s website, LinkedIn, and in the "State of Enterprise AI" annual report.
- **Visual Content:** Photographs and/or short video clips (3–5 minutes) of the collaboration, workshops, or interviews, featuring the Recipient's leadership and branding.
- **Public Announcements:** A joint press release announcing the Recipient as a Grant awardee.

> **The Nisol Promise (Recipient Data Protection):**  
> *We will never share your raw proprietary data, internal financials, or student/personnel records. We only share the **strategic insights, the journey, and the final outcomes**—never the underlying raw data.*

### 4. Intellectual Property (The "Zero Lock-In" Clause)
The strategic recommendations, architecture diagrams, and roadmaps produced during this grant remain the **sole intellectual property** of the Recipient. The Grantor retains the right to use the *methodology* and *framework* used to generate the recommendations for future clients, but the specific IP, code, and strategy tailored to the Recipient belongs entirely to the Recipient.

### 5. Confidentiality
Both parties agree to keep any sensitive operational or financial data shared during the sprint strictly confidential. This Agreement ensures a safe space for transparency.

### 6. Acceptance & Signatures
By signing below, both parties acknowledge they have read, understood, and agreed to this Partnership Acknowledgment.

## 🚀 OPERATIONAL INSTRUCTIONS (How to Deploy This)

1.  **Launch the Application (Week 1):**
    - Create a dedicated page on `nisolai.com/grants` with the Apple aesthetic.
    - Share it via a **LinkedIn post** (tagging top educators/NGO leaders) and send it directly to 20 hand-picked institutions via personalized email.

2.  **The "Juror" Invitation (Week 2):**
    - Send the scoring rubric (above) to your 3 external jurors. Host a 30-minute virtual meeting to review the top 10 applications together.

3.  **The Award Announcement (Week 4):**
    - Don't just send an email. **Video call** the winners to tell them they've been selected. Record the reaction (with their permission) for a "Winner Announcement" social clip.

4.  **The "Unboxing" of the Agreement (Week 5):**
    - Don't just email the PDF. Mail the physical, printed copy of this Agreement (cloth-bound, or at least premium cardstock) to the Recipient's headquarters. Have them sign it physically and return it. **This sets the tone that this is a premium, serious engagement.**

5.  **The Execution (Week 6-8):**
    - Run the 3 Grants **consecutively, not in parallel** (so your team isn't overwhelmed). Use the first one to refine the process, the second to optimize, and the third to perfect the "unboxing" video.

---

**Final Recommendation:** Print this Agreement on your premium letterhead and physically courier it to the grant winners. The physical act of signing a beautifully designed document is what makes them feel like "Apple awardees"—and that emotional commitment ensures they will eagerly participate in your marketing videos later.
