# Grant Rejection Email

## Keeping the Door Open — Graciously

---

### STRATEGIC INTENT

A rejection email is **not a dead end**—it's a **brand-building moment**. These enterprises *already* raised their hands. They showed intent. They filled out the application. They proved they have a budget and a problem.

**Your goal with this email:**
1. **Leave them feeling respected** (not rejected).
2. **Signal your own popularity** (scarcity reinforcement).
3. **Open a door** to a paid engagement or the next grant cycle.

---

## VERSION 1: THE "WAITLIST / NEXT CYCLE" EMAIL
*(For strong applicants who just missed the cut)*

---

**Subject:** Your application for the Nisol Enterprise Intelligence Grant

**To:** [First Name]

Dear [First Name],

Thank you for taking the time to apply for the **Nisol Enterprise Intelligence Grant**.

We received an extraordinary number of applications this cycle—far more than we anticipated. The independent jury reviewed each submission against our criteria of impact potential, leadership commitment, and readiness for transformation.

**After careful deliberation, we are unable to award the grant to [Organization Name] at this time.**

---

### Please understand: This is not a reflection on the quality of your organization or your mission.

The competition was exceptional, and we were genuinely impressed by [specific detail from their application—*e.g., "your approach to leveraging AI in supply chain"* or *"your commitment to student outcomes"*]. You were in the final round of consideration.

**What happens next:**

We are keeping **[Organization Name]** on our **Priority Waitlist** for the next grant cycle (opening in [Month, e.g., October 2026]).

- You will receive **early notification** before the next round opens.
- You will have **priority consideration** without needing to re-submit a full application.

---

### An alternative path forward

If your timeline cannot wait for the next cycle, we do offer a **paid Discovery pathway**—the same 7-day sprint, the same 15 deliverables, the same museum-quality dossier—available to qualified enterprises starting at ₹4,50,000.

We understand if this isn't the right fit right now. But if you'd like to explore it, simply reply "Yes" and we'll send over the scope of work.

---

We genuinely hope to work with [Organization Name] in the future. Your mission matters, and we believe there is a path forward—whether now or in the next cycle.

Warmly,

**[Founder/CMO Name]**
*Chief Marketing Officer, Nisol AI*

---

## VERSION 2: THE "UPSELL" REJECTION
*(For highly qualified enterprise applicants with clear budget)*

---

**Subject:** Your grant application — and an alternative path

**To:** [First Name]

Dear [First Name],

Thank you for your thoughtful application for the **Nisol Enterprise Intelligence Grant**.

The jury was genuinely impressed with [Organization Name]'s vision and readiness. Of the 40+ applications we received, yours ranked among the top tier.

**However, with only 3 grants available this quarter, we simply could not accommodate everyone who deserved it.**

---

### A different way to start

If the timeline you outlined in your application—[mention their stated urgency, e.g., "Q3 2026"]—requires immediate action, we can offer you access to the **exact same Nisol Discovery™ framework** under a fast-tracked engagement:

- **Timeline:** 7 days, just like the grant.
- **Deliverables:** 15 board-ready capability audits, DCF models, architecture blueprints.
- **Pricing:** ₹4,50,000 / $5,500 (the same value we grant to non-profits).
- **The difference:** You own the intellectual property outright, and you have full control over how the results are shared (or kept private).

---

### Why we're offering this

We believe [Organization Name] is ready for intelligence clarity. We don't want a bureaucratic grant cycle to be the reason you delay.

If you'd like to explore this path, simply reply "Yes" and I'll personally ensure we reserve a slot in our next available sprint window.

**Either way:** You are now part of the Nisol Circle. We will keep you updated on our research, reports, and future grant opportunities.

Warmly,

**[Founder/CMO Name]**
*Chief Marketing Officer, Nisol AI*

---

## 📋 EXECUTION NOTES

| Element | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Send Timing** | Within 48–72 hours of the "Winner" announcements. Don't let them wait. |
| **Personalization** | The **single most important line** is the personalized compliment (*"we were impressed by your approach to..."*). Always include this—it proves you actually read their application. |
| **Tracking** | Flag any recipients who **open this email more than 3 times**. They are highly interested. A Sales Development Rep should follow up with a phone call within 48 hours: *"I saw you opened the rejection email—I wanted to check if the paid pathway is something we should explore."* |
| **Waitlist Commitment** | If they reply "Waitlist me," add them to a dedicated CRM segment. 30 days before the next cycle, send them an exclusive "Early Access" invitation. |

## 🎯 REJECTION SEQUENCE (Optional Add-On)

If a rejected prospect doesn't reply to the first rejection email, consider this light follow-up (Day 10):

---

**Subject:** Quick follow-up on the grant decision

Hi [First Name],

Quick check-in—did you see my note about the grant decision?

I know the outcome wasn't what we both hoped for. But the offer to **fast-track the same 7-day sprint** (with full IP ownership) stands if your timeline requires it.

Alternatively, if you'd prefer to wait for the next grant cycle, we've added you to the Priority Waitlist. You'll hear from us in [Month].

Just reply "Waitlist" or "Fast-Track"—no need for a long email.

Warmly,

**[Founder/CMO Name]**
