## PRO TIP: THE "HANDOVER" CEREMONY

If you are meeting them in person for the Kickoff Call, **do not** hand them the dossier right away. Instead:
1. Place the physical dossier on the table, untouched, for the entire 90-minute kickoff.
2. At the very end of the meeting, slide it across the table and say:
> *"This is your Intelligence Dossier. It contains your complete architecture, your ROI models, and your roadmap. It's yours to keep. And the only thing we ask in return... is that you tell a few people about what we built together."*

This theatrical pause makes the physical deliverable feel like a sacred artifact—and ensures they remember the "partnership ask" long after you leave the room.
