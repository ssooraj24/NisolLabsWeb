## Why I like Fundoodata

You're not waiting for someone to discover you.

Instead, you can identify companies that match your ideal customer profile:

* 100–2000 employees
* Manufacturing
* IT Services
* Healthcare
* BFSI
* Logistics
* Professional Services

Then you proactively contact them.

This is exactly how many B2B consulting firms build their pipeline.

## LinkedIn's limitation

Suppose you post:

> "5 AI Use Cases for Manufacturing"

You might get:

* 8,000 impressions
* 120 likes
* 15 comments
* 2 inquiries

The reality is that **most viewers are not actively buying**.

A CEO who urgently wants AI consulting usually searches, asks for referrals, or responds to a direct, relevant outreach—not because they happened to see a post.

That doesn't mean LinkedIn is useless. It means it serves a different purpose:

* validates your expertise,
* reassures prospects after they've heard about you,
* and builds trust over time.

## My biggest recommendation

Don't think of Fundoodata as a database.

Think of it as your **sales engine**.

For example:

**Fundoodata**
↓
Filter manufacturing companies in Pune with 100–1000 employees.
↓
Visit their website.
↓
Identify potential AI opportunities.
↓
Send a personalized email.
↓
Connect on LinkedIn.
↓
Follow up with a call.
↓
Offer a **30-minute AI Opportunity Discussion**.
↓
Convert to a paid **AI Discovery Workshop**.

That's a much more targeted process than waiting for inbound interest.

## What I would avoid

Many people download 10,000 company records and send the same email to everyone.

That usually produces very poor results.

A better approach is to contact **20–30 carefully selected companies per day**, where you've spent a few minutes understanding their business and can mention something specific in your outreach.

## My recommendation for Nisol AI

If I were building Nisol AI from scratch today, I'd allocate my effort roughly like this:

* **50%** – Outbound using Fundoodata
* **20%** – Partnerships (software companies, ERP vendors, MSPs, cloud partners)

## One thing that can multiply your success

Instead of emailing:

> "We provide AI consulting."

Email with a **specific business outcome**.

For example:

* "We identified 6 AI opportunities that could reduce proposal creation time by 70% for manufacturing companies."
* "Based on your industry, we believe there are 4–5 AI use cases that could deliver measurable ROI. We'd be happy to walk you through them in a short discovery session."

That's much more compelling than a generic services pitch.

### One additional idea

Because you're already using Fundoodata, I would build a **target account selling** process rather than mass marketing.

For every target company, maintain a small profile containing:

* Company details
* Industry
* Likely AI use cases
* Decision makers
* Outreach history
* AI opportunity score
* Follow-up status

Over time, this becomes a valuable sales asset for Nisol AI and lets you focus your effort where the probability of winning business is highest.
