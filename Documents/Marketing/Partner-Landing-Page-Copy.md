## [SECTION: FAQ]

### Headline
## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does it cost to join?**
Nothing. The partner program is completely free. No joining fee, no annual fee, no hidden costs.

**Q: Do I need technical skills?**
No. Referral Partners simply make introductions. Nisol AI handles everything — sales, implementation, and support.

**Q: How are commissions tracked?**
Every partner gets a unique referral link and access to a real-time dashboard. You can see every lead, deal stage, and payout at any time.

**Q: What if I refer a lead you're already talking to?**
We'll tell you upfront. If our team has already engaged the prospect, we'll notify you within 48 hours of your deal registration. No surprises.

**Q: How long is my deal protected?**
90 days from the date you register the deal. If the deal hasn't closed in 90 days, we'll discuss an extension on a case-by-case basis.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
Monthly, net-30. Once a referred client pays us, your commission is processed within 30 days. Minimum payout threshold is ₹10,000 ($120).

**Q: What happens after Year 1?**
Your commission drops from 30% to 20% on renewals — but it keeps paying for as long as the client stays with Nisol AI. If you help upsell a client, you earn 30% on the new incremental revenue.

**Q: Can I partner from outside India?**
Absolutely. We're actively looking for partners in India and the Middle East (especially Dubai/UAE). If you have enterprise relationships in either market, we want to talk.

**Q: What's the difference between Referral and Implementation Partners?**
Referral Partners make introductions and earn commissions with zero delivery work. Implementation Partners co-sell and sometimes help deliver Nisol AI solutions — they earn higher commissions and get deeper support.
