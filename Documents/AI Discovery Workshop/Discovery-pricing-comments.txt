Yes, I remember the discussion around this section.

The conclusion was:

### What we wanted to keep

✅ The **"Why Nisol Discovery"** section

✅ The **comparison with traditional consulting**

✅ The concepts:

* Executive Speed
* Proprietary Methodology
* Board-Ready Outcomes
* Enterprise Expertise

✅ The message that:

> Nisol AI helps organizations move from AI uncertainty to an implementation roadmap in days instead of months.

---

### What we wanted to remove

❌ ₹75,000 / Day pricing

❌ Any explicit day-rate pricing

❌ Any consulting-style billing language

❌ Anything that makes Nisol AI look like a consulting company charging for workshop hours

Because we agreed:

> Nisol AI is not a consulting company.
>
> Nisol AI is an end-to-end AI Transformation Partner that discovers, designs, implements, integrates, governs, monitors and optimizes AI solutions.

---

### Bigger issue with current headline

Current headline:

> Why Executive Discovery is ₹75,000 / Day

This immediately creates objections:

* Too expensive?
* Why ₹75,000?
* Comparing price before value
* Looks like a consultant selling workshop days

Instead, we wanted to sell **business outcomes**, not workshop fees.
