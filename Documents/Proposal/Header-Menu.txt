### One thing I would avoid

I would **remove the standalone ROI Calculator button** from the header. It's an excellent tool, but in the header it competes with your main call to action. Instead, make the **ROI Calculator** a prominent section within the **Discovery** page and also feature it on the homepage as an interactive lead-generation tool. That way, every visitor understands it as part of your proprietary Discovery methodology rather than as an isolated calculator. This also keeps the header cleaner and more focused on guiding visitors toward a **Discovery Call**, which is the conversation that ultimately leads to consulting engagements.
